# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone others can work with. Assume the person you are replying to is
competent, busy, and reading in their second language.

## What that means here

- **Argue about the work, not the person.** "This breaks the contract for
  existing consumers" is a review. "You clearly did not read the spec" is not.
- **Say what you measured.** Disagreement is cheap and useful when it comes with
  a reproduction; it is expensive and useless when it comes with an adjective.
- **Take no for an answer, and give reasons with yours.** Not every good change
  belongs in this repository. A maintainer who declines owes you the reason.
- **English is the working language, and most of us are not native speakers.**
  Correcting someone's grammar in a technical thread is off-topic.

## Not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, and unwelcome attention of any kind. Publishing
someone's private information. Sustained disruption of a discussion. Behaviour
that would make a reasonable person stop contributing.

## Reporting

Write to **conduct@phimajor.com**. Reports go to the maintainers and are not
discussed publicly. Tell us what happened and what you would like to happen
next; we will tell you what we decided and why.

Maintainers who break these rules are held to them more strictly, not less.
